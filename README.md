## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I am an **iOS Engineer** with **6+ years** of professional experience building large-scale
production applications — including the connected-car apps for **Nissan** and **Infiniti**.

- 📱 Deep native iOS: **Swift**, **SwiftUI**, **UIKit**, **Combine**, **AVFoundation**.
- 🧱 Modular architecture at scale — internal Swift packages, MVVM, testable boundaries.
- 🌍 Multi-brand, multi-region delivery: one codebase shipping branded apps per market.
- 🌐 Beyond mobile: I build and ship full products with **Next.js**, **React**, and **React Native** — [Hyoor](https://hyoor.am) is mine end to end.
- 🌱 Currently going deeper on **backend & databases** and **AI / LLM application development**.
